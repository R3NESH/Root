# The legal lane is open — and it is a real advantage

**Fact.** Supreme Court, March 2020: Section 37 of the Architects Act does not bar unregistered
persons from practising architecture. It bars only the use of the *title* "architect."

Meanwhile the Council of Architecture's Scale of Charges sets a **mandatory minimum** of 7.5% of
cost of works for individual houses. Registered architects cannot legally go below it.

**Consequence.** Incumbents have a statutory price floor. This product has no licensing
requirement and no floor. That asymmetry is the clearest structural advantage in the whole brief
— clearer than [[layout-stability]], which is only a guess until it is measured.

> [!danger] Copy rule
> Never use the word "architect" in product copy, marketing, or UI strings.
> The title is protected; the practice is not. Enforce it in review.
