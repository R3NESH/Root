# The field is crowded but shallow

**Fact.** At least nine free tools already do questionnaire → Vaastu-aware 2D plan for Indian
plot sizes:

Forjit · Fix to Finish · Naksha AI · VastuAgent.ai · AI Cadbull · GrehYug · Coohom India ·
DesignDrafter

GrehYug goes further: 3D, DXF export, cost estimation, WhatsApp delivery.

**Consequence.** UI polish alone is not a differentiator. Nine tools already clear that bar.
Whatever this product wins on has to be something none of them do — the current bet is
[[layout-stability]], and that bet is [[claim-most-likely-wrong|explicitly flagged as the one
most likely to be wrong]].
