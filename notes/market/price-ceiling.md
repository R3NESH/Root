# Price ceiling is low

**Fact.** Indian rates: a floor plan runs ₹4,000–6,000 per floor. A full set with structural and
working drawings, single floor, runs ₹12,000–20,000. Roughly $140–240 for everything.

**Consequence.** Unit economics must work at a ₹3,000–5,000 ticket. No enterprise SaaS
assumptions — no seats, no per-project pricing tiers, no sales motion. This is why
[[autodcr-owns-municipal-scrutiny]] closing the B2B door is not merely inconvenient but
structural.

Note the tension with [[architects-act-legal-lane]]: registered architects have a *mandatory
floor* of 7.5% of cost of works, so on a ₹20 lakh build they cannot legally quote under
₹1.5 lakh. The ceiling above is the informal-market rate, not theirs.

**Untested.** ₹500 has never been asked of anyone: [[q-does-anyone-pay]].
