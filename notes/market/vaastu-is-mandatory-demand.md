# Vaastu is a hard requirement, not a nice-to-have

**Fact.** NoBroker survey, 12,546 respondents: 73% check Vaastu before buying.
Hyderabad 77%, Chennai 78%, Bengaluru 76%. A separate Hyderabad study puts it at ~82% as a major
decision factor.

**Consequence.** Vaastu is a gate, not a garnish. It goes in as solver constraints — see
[[vaastu-as-constraints]] — because a plan that violates it is not a worse plan, it is a rejected
plan.

Hyderabad numbers matter most here: the first real test plot is in Kandi, Telangana. See
[[build-order]].
