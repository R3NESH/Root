# TG-bPASS kills the permit-automation angle

**Fact.** TG-bPASS grants instant online building permission for residential plots up to
500 sq m and 10 m height. Plots up to 75 sq yards (ground or G+1) need no building permission and
no occupancy certificate at all — a ₹1 token registration. Written into the TS-bPASS Act 2020,
Section 7.

**Consequence.** Do not build permit-submission features. For the target segment the pain has
been legislated away. Listed in [[rejected-approaches]].

**Unresolved.** What fraction of the real market sits *above* the threshold?
See [[q-market-above-bpass-threshold]] — if it is tiny, the permit-adjacent framing has no market
at all, and every remaining pitch has to stand on the plan itself.

Source: [[HANDOFF]] §2
