## Findings

- **[[vaastu-and-connectivity-drop-on-edit]] — blocking.** `effective_vaastu`/`effective_connect`
  are set to `False` whenever `prev` is passed, and `useSolve.ts` sends `prev` on every solve
  after the first. So Vaastu quadrants and hall connectivity hold for the **first solve only**.
  Reproduced live: `vaastu_constraints_applied` goes `[3 rules] → []` after one edit, and
  reachability goes **6/6 → 2/6**. Kitchen placed at `(0,0)` — north-west, the exact placement
  the constraint exists to forbid. Promoted to its own note per the [[workflow]] promotion rule.
- **The suite cannot see it.** `test_vaastu.py` never passes `prev` (0 occurrences);
  `test_stability.py:80` does pass `prev` with `apply_vaastu=True` but asserts only status and
  solve time. The one test on the broken path checks the two properties that still work.
- **[[duplicated-geometry]].** `openings`, `wall_thickness_in`, `entrance_edge` and
  `rooms_reachable` appear in the frontend **only** as type declarations in `lib/solve.ts` —
  zero uses. `Scene.tsx` re-derives doors itself. Third duplication, and one live disagreement:
  interior walls are 5 in on the backend, 4.5 in in the renderer.
- **Entrance fires in 38% of layouts.** `add_entrance()` returns `None` when the hub touches no
  exterior wall, which is most of the time with Vaastu on. [[step-5-vaastu]]'s recorded gap is
  half-closed on the backend and fully open in the product.
- **Fill is unchanged by the connectivity fix.** Mean 39.6%, min 26.0% across 24 mixes. Rooms
  solve near their catalog minimums because nothing rewards a larger room. Ceiling for the
  standard 6-room mix is 66.2%, so the target is the catalog, not the envelope.
- **Test count is 25, not the 23 in [[test-baseline]].** `test_api.py` 10, `test_solver.py` 6,
  `test_stability.py` 4, `test_vaastu.py` 5. Two tests landed with the first commit and were
  never recorded.
- **The suite is wall-clock flaky under load.** First run today: 2 failed, 23 passed in 44.4 s,
  both failures in `test_stability.py` with `status == 'UNKNOWN'`. Re-run on a quiet machine:
  **25 passed in 11.5 s and 15.2 s**, and `test_stability.py` alone passed 3/3 times. The 0.4 s
  interactive cap is wall-clock, so CPU contention turns a latency budget into a correctness
  failure. Not a code regression — but a suite that goes red under load will get ignored.
- **Vault drift.** [[Home]] says "no commit" (there are 7); `backend/README.md` said `vaastu/`,
  `envelope/` and `api/` were "not started" and tests "5/5"; [[codebase-map]] was missing
  `connectivity.py`, `interiorDetails.ts`, `walkthrough.ts` and four components; and
  [[rooms-do-not-form-a-house]] pointed at a `step-6-walkthrough` note that did not exist.
  All fixed today.

### Three bugs found by measuring rather than reasoning

- **"Exterior wall" meant the plot, not the building.** A twelve-room house was INFEASIBLE on a
  40×60 and feasible on a 30×40 — backwards. Room maximums mean the house does not span a large
  plot. Now derived from the built footprint.
- **The tree contradicted the kitchen/bathroom taboo.** INFEASIBLE in 13 ms, no diagnosis.
- **The tree contradicted Vaastu.** The fan-out cap parented the pooja room to the kitchen — NE
  and SE, opposite corners — which took a real 30×40 from 3 Vaastu rules to **zero**. This is
  the one that mattered: it is the plot size the product targets and the constraint the market
  treats as mandatory.

## End of day — two corrections from the user

- **Time-of-day sun removed.** Built, correct, and not asked for. Logged in
  [[rejected-approaches]] with the reason, so it is not re-proposed as an easy win.
- **"The walls have no doors."** Not a rendering bug. The backend on port 8000 was **yesterday's
  process**, started 2026-08-24 18:04, still serving `openings: []`. Because the renderer now
  draws doors only from the solver's openings — deliberately, with no fallback, since a fallback
  is exactly what [[duplicated-geometry]] was — an out-of-date backend produces a sealed house.
  Restarting `uvicorn` fixed it: 8 door leaves, 6 windows, 1 entrance.
  
  **The real defect was that this failed silently.** `useSolve` now detects an all-empty
  `openings` response and the header says "Solver is out of date — restart the backend".

- **The stability benchmark had gone blind, and it nearly cost a correct conclusion.**
  `bench_stability` reported 0 displacement *without* the drift objective, which reads as
  [[claim-most-likely-wrong]] finally dying. Isolating sixteen configurations showed the
  opposite: the objective works in every one of them, and its old 5-room/30x40 scenario had
  simply become over-determined by the new constraints. Given slack the effect is **larger**
  than in August — 12,423 in total drift, worst jump 2,877 in (240 ft), against 0 in with the
  objective. The bench now uses a scenario with room to move. A benchmark whose null hypothesis
  cannot move is not a benchmark.

## Late changes — scope pulled back

- **Four room kinds removed** on the user's call: parking porch, sit-out, staircase, utility.
  Catalog is now 7 — hall, dining, kitchen, bedroom, bathroom, pooja, store. The `open_sided`
  concept went with them, since the porch and sit-out were its only users and unreachable code
  is a trap. The removed kinds now come back in `unknown_room_names` rather than being silently
  placed. [[test-baseline]] 41 -> 40: the open-sided test went with the behaviour it guarded.
- **Auto-Furnish Interiors checkbox added.** Checked by default; unchecked renders the bare
  shell — no furniture, no ceiling fans, no curtains. Walls, doors, windows, floors and lighting
  stay, because those are what the solver actually decided. The bare view is arguably the more
  honest one for judging a plan.
- **Camera bug avoided.** The toggle first landed on the camera-reframe effect's dependency
  array, which would have yanked the view every time someone ticked the box — the same defect
  caught in review on 2026-08-24. Moved to the geometry rebuild, where it belongs.
