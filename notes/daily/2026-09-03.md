# Daily Note — 2026-09-03

## Work Accomplished Today

### 1. Walls as First-Class Objects & BIM Engine
- Implemented `backend/solver/walls.py` deriving walls post-solve from room bounds and shared partition runs:
  - Every wall gets a persistent ID, endpoints `(x0, y0) -> (x1, y1)`, thickness (9 in exterior vs 4.5 in partition), exterior flag, bounded room indices, and hosted openings.
  - Eliminated duplicate partition door tracking; a shared wall hosts interior connecting doors once.
  - See [[walls-as-objects]].

### 2. Engineering Bill of Quantities (BOQ) & Cost Estimation
- Implemented `backend/solver/quantities.py` counting takeoff directly from wall objects:
  - Carpet area, built-up area, wall run (ft), gross/net wall area (sq ft), masonry volume (cu ft).
  - Physical brick count from standard void/joint geometry: 434/m³ traditional brick (230×110×70) or 500/m³ IS 1077 modular brick (190×90×90).
  - Mortar volume, 12 mm internal / 15 mm external plaster, door/window schedules.
  - No fake rupee totals in the backend; caller supplies rates.
- Built interactive frontend `BOQCostModal.tsx` and `lib/boqEngine.ts`:
  - Economy (₹1,650/sq ft), Premium (₹2,150/sq ft), and Luxury (₹2,950/sq ft) quality tiers.
  - Itemized breakdowns for civil, masonry, finishes, MEP, and labor with cement bag, steel, sand, aggregate, and brick tallies.
  - Integrated into the architectural export sheet in `BlueprintExportModal.tsx`.

### 3. NBC 2016 Room Sizing & Wet Room Vent Separation
- Refactored `backend/solver/rooms.py` to National Building Code of India 2016 minimums:
  - Hall 10×12 ft, kitchen 7×8 ft, bathroom 4×6 ft, entrance 4×4 ft.
  - Unlocked feasible solves for compact Indian plots (1BHK on 20×30, 2BHK on 25×40, 3BHK on 30×40).
- Separated vent minimums from window minimums in `backend/solver/connectivity.py`:
  - `VENT_MIN_WALL_IN = 36` vs `WINDOW_MIN_WALL_IN = 60`, allowing compact 4 ft bathrooms to ventilate properly without breaking exterior wall constraints.
  - See [[room-sizes-from-code]].

### 4. Compact Footprint Objective (CP-SAT)
- Fixed scattered pavilions bug where CP-SAT returned loose L-shaped layouts with up to 28% internal void:
  - Added `realism.footprint_perimeter_term()` with `COMPACT_WEIGHT = 120`.
  - Linear half-perimeter penalty pulls rooms tightly together, reducing internal void to 5–7% while maintaining fast cold-solve proof times.
  - See [[compact-footprint]].
