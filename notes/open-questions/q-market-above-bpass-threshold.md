# Q4 — What fraction of the market is above the TG-bPASS threshold?

The threshold is 500 sq m or 10 m height.

**If the fraction is tiny, the permit-adjacent framing has no market at all.** Every pitch then
has to stand on the quality of the plan itself, which puts all the weight on
[[claim-most-likely-wrong]].

Unverified.

Note the scale involved: 500 sq m is ~5,380 sq ft, roughly 598 sq yards. The plot sizes the whole
product is designed around — 20×30 through 50×80 ft, i.e. 600 to 4,000 sq ft
([[ui-principles]]) — are **all comfortably under it**. So the working assumption is that the
answer is "small," and the permit angle stays dead. Confirm before anyone writes copy that
implies otherwise.

Source: [[HANDOFF]] §7.4
